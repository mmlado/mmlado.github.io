Mladen Milankovic

mmlado - walleteer. Air-gapped wallets that do not phone home.

Freelance engineer. Author of Keycard Pal, an air-gapped wallet for Status Keycard, and of the keycard-py and keycard-nim SDKs. Contributed two instructions to the Status Keycard JavaCard applet: GET CHALLENGE (in production) and an ECDH key-agreement instruction (merged, shipping in v4). Delivered two Rust RFP libraries for Logos spel.

Independent, open to work in the privacy space.

I believe sovereignty begins the moment someone realizes: you don't have to accept the default.
